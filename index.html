Official Business Profile

About Niranjan Electronics

Established in 1998 by proprietor Niranjan Chaudhary, Niranjan Electronics is a leading district-level electrical and electronics enterprise based in Budhaura, Alauli, Khagaria, Bihar – 848203, India. Operating on a full district-wide scale, the business supplies retail and wholesale electrical products across the entire Khagaria district.

As an Official Distributor of Surya, Niranjan Electronics holds deep expertise in electrical wiring materials and complete residential and commercial wiring solutions. Across 12 comprehensive product categories, the enterprise deals in electrical wires and cables, modular switches and switchgear, LED lighting, fans and cooling solutions, home and kitchen appliances, televisions and audio systems, DTH accessories, mobile and computer peripherals, wiring installation materials, power backup systems, water pumps, and solar lighting. Alongside its core electrical distribution network, Niranjan Electronics also operates Bisleri drinking water distribution as a separate business activity across Khagaria district.

Operating 24 hours a day, 7 days a week (24/7), Niranjan Electronics stands for decades of technical expertise, district-wide supply reliability, and long-term trust.
